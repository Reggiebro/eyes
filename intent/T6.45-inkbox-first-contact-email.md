## Why

Primitive refuses every first send to a normal domain until it grants the org `send_to_any_domain` (T6.44; requested 2026-10-18, not self-serve). As a result, owners never got their control link, and ops sign-in codes and worker email could not reach gmail.com. The Inkbox dispatcher identity `eyes-dispatch` already has a mailbox, and the server already holds its key. Inkbox sends to any domain: a test from that mailbox to the operator's allowlisted address on 2026-10-18 came back `delivered` (sent through SES; inkboxmail.com SPF passes). Cloudflare Email Service and a transactional provider would each need new DNS, keys and secrets, so Inkbox is the fastest working path.

## What changed

- `packages/comms/src/inkbox.ts` `createInkboxEmail`: the core `Email` interface over Inkbox REST `POST /api/v1/mail/mailboxes/{mailbox}/messages`. It looks up the mailbox from the identity once (and again after a failed lookup) and passes Reply-To once `OutboundEmail` carries `reply_to`. A refusal throws `InkboxSendError(<status> <code>)`, without the recipient. It sends no attachments.
- `packages/comms/src/primitive.ts` `createPrimitiveEmail(env, firstContact?)`: a `403 recipient_not_allowed`, or an async `gate_denied`, hands the same message to `firstContact`. Other errors still throw. Replies and photo answers stay on Primitive, and once Primitive grants any-domain sending nothing reaches the fallback.
- `apps/mcp/src/main.ts`: with live Inkbox, Primitive gets Inkbox email as its first-contact sender. No new env var.
- `apps/mcp/src/server.ts`: `/status` shows "Email: live (first contact via Inkbox until Primitive enables any-domain sending)". Without Inkbox it still says "replies only"; with `PRIMITIVE_SEND_ANY_DOMAIN=1` it shows plain "live".

## Verification

- `inkbox.test.ts`: request URL, header and body, including Reply-To; a single mailbox lookup, retried after a failure; 429/403/502 error names; an identity with no mailbox.
- `primitive.test.ts`: a 403 `recipient_not_allowed` and a `gate_denied` both reach the fallback with the same message; a 429 is rethrown and not handed over.
- `server.test.ts`: the three `/status` email lines.
- Live probe before the code change: an Inkbox CLI send from `eyes-dispatch` to the operator's allowlisted email returned `sent`, then `delivered`.

## Known gaps

- Inkbox caps the org at 100 new recipients a day, shared with nothing else today. A cap hit is a send error (an Errors-tab issue), not a silent drop.
- The free plan adds a "Sent via Inkbox" footer. inkboxmail.com publishes DMARC `p=none`, so spam placement depends on the recipient's provider; inbox placement was not checked.
- Owner email now comes from inkboxmail.com, so an owner's reply lands in the Inkbox mailbox, which Eyes does not read.
