## Why
A worker had two private pages: /me with the 5 newest claims and /w/earnings with totals and the 50 newest. Older tasks could not be found, and the payout label rule had two renderers. Decision: ADR-029.

## What changed
- `GET /w/me/tasks` (bearer code as `GET /w/me`): totals plus one page of 20 tasks, newest first, keyset cursor; `q` searches only what a row shows (question, place, date in the browser's `tz`, `$3.00`); `status` filters paid, pending, review, expired.
- Engine `workerTasks` over the new RPC `eyes_worker_tasks_page` (migration 026); `workerEarnings` and `WorkerSetup.tasks`/`earnings_url` are gone.
- /me ends with "Your tasks": totals, search box, filter chips, "Show more".
- `GET /w/earnings/:token` answers 301 to `/me#c=<token>`; the paid text links /me ("Your tasks: <link>").

## Verification
- `engine.int.test.ts`: paging 20 at a time, malformed cursor, search by question, place, local date and amount (never cents, ids or UTC time), each filter, 401 for bad codes.
- `server.test.ts`: `/w/me/tasks` passes the bearer code and shown filters, drops unknown ones, 401 without a code. `payments/src/index.test.ts`: `/w/earnings/:token` 301 to /me with and without a well-formed token.
- `me.test.ts` and `e2e/me.spec.ts`: list rendering, search, filters, "Show more", empty and error states.
- See the commit message for test counts.

## Known gaps
- Search is an `ilike` scan over the worker's own claims; add a trigram index if a worker reaches many thousands of tasks.
